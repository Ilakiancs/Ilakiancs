I'm an SRE intern at **Sysco LABS** and a CS undergrad at the **University of Westminster (IIT)**, based in Colombo. I build AI products end to end: fine-tuning open models, wiring them into real apps, and keeping them running in production.

Most recently I built **[OptiLearn](https://github.com/Ilakiancs/OptiLearn)** for the Gemma 4 Good Hackathon 2026. It turns one teacher's laptop into an offline AI tutor for a whole classroom, using Gemma 4 fine-tuned with Unsloth.
